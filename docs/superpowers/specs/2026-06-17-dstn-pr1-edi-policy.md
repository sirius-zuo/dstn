## i) Offeror's anti-discriminatory/EDI policies

Stratos / DEC Foundation maintains a written workplace anti-discrimination policy, aligned with the Canadian Human Rights Act, prohibiting discrimination on race, ethnicity, religion, age, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, family status, and disability across hiring, compensation, promotion, and termination. The policy applies to all employees and contractors.

The company is committed to improving access to leadership and economic participation for Women, Indigenous Peoples, Racialized persons, 2SLGBTQI+ individuals, and Persons with disabilities. As an early-stage company, formal tracking of team composition against these groups does not yet exist — an identified gap.

**Plan to meet targets:** (1) begin formally tracking team composition this contract period, (2) establish a baseline by Month 2, and (3) set year-over-year representation targets for hiring and leadership, reviewed annually by senior management alongside D&I training completion.
