## ii) Offeror's recruitment strategy and hiring processes

Recruitment actively reduces barriers for underrepresented candidates: job postings are distributed through channels reaching Indigenous professionals, women in tech, and persons with disabilities (Technation's Diversity in Tech, Indigenous Works, provincial equity-in-tech programs); blind resume review is applied at initial screening; an internship/co-op pipeline with Montreal-area universities (Concordia, UQAM) prioritizes equity-deserving candidates; interviews use standardized rubrics to reduce evaluator subjectivity.

Leadership/ownership composition and workforce baselines are not yet formally tracked, reflecting the company's early stage — an identified gap addressed under the EDI policy plan.

**Specific target:** given DSTN's relevance to Indigenous-owned and women-owned supplier certification (CCIB, WBE Canada), the company prioritizes outreach to Indigenous and women candidates for roles supporting these credential types, tracked from the Month 2 baseline.
