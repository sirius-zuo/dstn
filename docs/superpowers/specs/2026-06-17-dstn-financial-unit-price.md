## Fixed All-Inclusive Single Innovation Unit Price (Goods and/or Services)

**$350,000.00 CAD**

## Rationale, including number of units proposed for the ISC testing scenario

Number of units proposed for the ISC testing scenario: **1**.

**Unit definition:** one unit is a single, complete DSTN deployment — the Aries Issuer Agent, Data Aggregation Service, Supplier Portal, Passkey Registry, TEE Holder Agent, Verifier Widget, and Verification API, configured and licensed for one Testing Department (PSPC). DSTN is delivered as an integrated software platform rather than discrete physical goods, so "one unit" is defined as one full department-scoped deployment, consistent with the SaaS-per-department licensing model the Offeror uses commercially.

**Rationale for $350,000 CAD:** this reflects the Offeror's standard commercial unit price for a large federal department deployment (Business Plan: $300,000–$400,000 CAD/year for departments of PSPC's or Shared Services Canada's scale), set at the midpoint of that range. The price covers the deployed platform's ongoing license to operate — automated issuance, holder, verification, and revocation services — for the duration of use by the Testing Department, separate from the one-time hardening, installation, training, support, and testing/analysis costs already itemized in the overall Financial Proposal. This unit price represents what Canada (or any future department customer) would pay on an ongoing commercial basis for one deployment of the innovation once it reaches full commercial availability, distinct from the bounded, one-time ISC test contract value.
