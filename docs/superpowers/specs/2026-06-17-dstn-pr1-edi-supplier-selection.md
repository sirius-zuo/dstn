## How diversity and inclusion are factored into the Offeror's supplier selection methods

When selecting subcontractors and vendors, Stratos / DEC Foundation factors EDI alongside technical and commercial criteria: shortlisting gives preference, all else equal, to vendors that are Indigenous-owned, women-owned, or certified diverse suppliers under recognized Canadian programs (e.g., WBE Canada, CCIB). For this engagement, subcontracts will solicit bids from diverse suppliers before awarding solely to non-diverse vendors.

An "open-source first" preference also favors community-built alternatives over incumbent vendors, reducing spend concentration in non-diverse technology conglomerates.

**Engagement with underrepresented communities:** DSTN's architecture extends supplier credentialing to Indigenous Business Certification (CCIB) and Women-Owned Business Certification (WBE Canada) in future phases, giving the company direct, ongoing engagement with these bodies and their member businesses as part of the product roadmap, not only as a procurement preference.
