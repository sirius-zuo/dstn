## Rationale for the Total Price, including a breakdown of its components

Total Price reflects the full 6-month delivery team (1 Technical Lead, 2 Protocol Engineers, 2 Application Engineers, 2 Infrastructure/TEE Engineers — 42 person-months) executing the milestone plan in the Technical Proposal, plus external testing and assessment costs.

**Innovation ($1,495,000):** the core engineering build-out and hardening of the existing TRL-7 prototype to TRL 9 — `did:stratos` W3C finalization, the automated Data Aggregation Service/issuance pipeline, the Supplier Portal, Passkey Registry, TEE Holder Agent, Verifier Widget, Verification API, and TEE hardware attestation integration (Months 1–6). This is the largest line item as it covers the majority of engineering labor across all three contract phases.

**Installation ($184,000):** deployment and configuration of the system into the PSPC-adjacent test environment using the existing Kubernetes manifests, including network/firewall integration, TLS/DNS setup, and environment-specific configuration (Months 5–6).

**Training ($115,000):** onboarding materials and hands-on training for PSPC test participants and administrators on the Supplier Portal, Admin Dashboard, and Verifier Widget, plus documentation in English and French.

**Support Services ($276,000):** operational support, monitoring, and incident response throughout the test period, including the 30-day stability run, covering uptime monitoring, alerting response, and issue triage.

**Other Direct Costs ($230,000):** third-party security audit and the Treasury Board-aligned security/privacy assessment, TEE hardware attestation validation, performance/load testing, and analysis and reporting of test results (Months 5–6).

This breakdown is a proposed starting allocation; final scope and pricing will be negotiated with the Testing Department per the Contract Award Process.
