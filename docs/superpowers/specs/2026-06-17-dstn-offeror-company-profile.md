## 1. Brief history of the Offeror's company and structure of the business

Stratos was founded in 2021 to build an open, decentralized "Dcloud" infrastructure — blockchain, storage, compute, and database — as an alternative to centralized cloud providers for Web3 developers. The company operates under the DEC (Decentralized) Foundation structure: a foundation-governed entity that stewards the Stratos protocol, network economics, and open-source codebase, rather than a single closed corporate hierarchy. This model lets Stratos issue and govern its native STOS token, coordinate an independent global network of resource node operators, and maintain neutral, non-custodial infrastructure that no single party controls.

Since 2021, Stratos has operated a production mainnet (Stratos Blockchain) with real commercial traffic, evolving its architecture (now "Stratos 2.0") from general Web3 Dcloud infrastructure toward a security-hardened platform for trusted, agentic AI and identity workloads — the same TEE compute, decentralized storage/database, and blockchain DID anchoring that underpins DSTN. The Canadian delivery team operates as Stratos / DEC Foundation, based in Montreal, Quebec, meeting the solicitation's Canadian-content and Canadian-employment requirements.

## 2. The company's operations and types of product or service offerings

Stratos operates a production-grade decentralized cloud network ("Dcloud") with four core service layers consumed directly by customers and developers:

- **Blockchain** — the Stratos Blockchain anchors decentralized identifiers (DIDs), schemas, and an immutable audit/transaction layer, secured by the company's own Proof-of-Traffic consensus mechanism.
- **Decentralized Storage** — a scalable, self-balancing, traffic-driven storage network for structured and unstructured data of any size.
- **Decentralized Computation** — an edge computing network offering low-latency processing with built-in Trusted Execution Environment (TEE) capability at each node.
- **Decentralized Database** — a schema-less, high-availability, no-single-point-of-failure database with an immutable mode powering oracle and data-aggregation use cases.

These layers are consumed today by Web3 developers (DeFi, GameFi, NFT/art, social, DePIN) and priced via the native STOS token. Stratos's newest offering, Stratos OS, packages the same TEE-backed compute, identity, and storage primitives into a "sovereign AI agent" execution environment — capability-based security, an identity mesh linking agents to hardware-verified credentials, and a hardware vault for signing material — the direct technical basis for DSTN's credential issuance, holder, and verification services.
