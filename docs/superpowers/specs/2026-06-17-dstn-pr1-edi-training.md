## Training and communication available to educate the Offeror's workforce on diversity and inclusion

All Stratos / DEC Foundation employees complete diversity and inclusion training covering: unconscious bias awareness (required at onboarding, with an annual refresher); inclusive leadership (mandatory for team leads and hiring managers, covering equitable evaluation practices); and respectful workplace conduct (harassment, accommodation, and reporting procedures aligned with Canadian legal requirements).

Training resources are sourced from recognized Canadian providers, including the Canadian Centre for Diversity and Inclusion, with completion tracked in annual HR review.

**Communication tools:** the anti-discrimination policy and training materials are available to all staff via internal HR documentation, team leads receive periodic refresher communications, and a confidential reporting channel exists for D&I or conduct concerns. As a small, early-stage company, formal completion-rate tracking and a dedicated D&I resource library are planned enhancements during this contract.
