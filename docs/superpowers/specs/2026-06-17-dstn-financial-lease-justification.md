## Is the proposed pricing based on a lease, rental, or licence model rather than a purchase?

**Yes.**

## Justification, lease/licence price, and equivalent purchase price

Justification for a licence/subscription model rather than an outright purchase:

DSTN is not a standalone, transferable asset Canada could take ownership of and operate independently. Credential issuance, holder, and verification functions depend on continuous, live operation of the Stratos Dcloud network — the blockchain (`did:stratos` anchoring), decentralized storage (revocation registry), and TEE compute nodes that perform credential signing and attestation. This infrastructure is a shared, multi-tenant network operated by Stratos / DEC Foundation, not a static deployable artifact; it cannot be "purchased" and handed over for independent GC operation without replicating an entire decentralized network, which is neither technically practical nor commercially intended. The same dependency applies to ongoing security patching, `did:stratos` protocol updates, and TEE attestation validity, all of which require the Offeror's continued operational involvement.

Accordingly, the proposed pricing is an annual licence/subscription ($350,000 CAD per unit, as indicated in the Single Innovation Unit Price), covering continued access to the issuance, holder, verification, and revocation services for one Testing Department for one year, renewable thereafter.

**Equivalent purchase price: $1,400,000 CAD.** This is calculated as a 4x multiple of the annual licence price, a standard SaaS-to-perpetual-licence conversion reflecting the net present value of a typical multi-year department relationship (approximately 4–5 years) discounted for the fact that a "purchase" would still require an ongoing service agreement with Stratos for infrastructure access, security patching, and protocol maintenance, since full independent operation by Canada is not feasible given the shared-network architecture described above. This figure is provided as the form's required equivalent purchase reference price; the Offeror's recommended commercial path remains the annual licence model.
