## Certifications, licenses, regulatory approvals, or equivalent frameworks

DSTN is not a regulated product (not a medical device, controlled good, or safety-critical hardware), so no statutory licence such as an MDL/MDEL applies. Safe operational usability is instead supported by adherence to the following open standards/frameworks, several with formal commitments during the contract:

| Standard/Framework | Number | Issuing Body | Status/Date | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W3C VC Data Model 2.0 | N/A (open spec) | W3C | Adopted | Defines the credential format DSTN issues |
| W3C DID Core 1.0 | N/A | W3C | `did:key` adopted; `did:stratos` submission Month 1–2 | Standardizes issuer/holder/verifier identity resolution |
| WebAuthn Level 3 / FIDO2-CTAP2 | N/A | W3C / FIDO Alliance | Adopted | Governs passkey auth; removes password/wallet-seed risk |
| AnonCreds 1.0 / Hyperledger Aries RFCs | N/A | Hyperledger Foundation | Adopted | Privacy-preserving issuance protocol, same stack as BC Gov production systems |
| WCAG 2.1 AA | N/A | W3C | Target by Month 3–4 | Accessibility of Portal and Widget |
| Official Languages Act compliance | N/A | Government of Canada | Target by Month 3–4 | Bilingual EN/FR interfaces |
| Treasury Board Directive on Privacy — aligned assessment | TBD | Self-assessed, reviewed with PSPC | Planned Month 5–6 | Reviews data handling, encryption, access controls before test scales |

No government-issued licence or certification is required because DSTN does not fall into a regulated category. Safe usability is instead demonstrated through conformance to the international open standards above plus a Treasury Board-aligned security/privacy assessment scheduled as a contract deliverable before expanded test partner exposure.
